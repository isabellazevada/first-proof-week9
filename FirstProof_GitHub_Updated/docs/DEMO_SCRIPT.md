# Demo recording guide | 3 minutes + 30 seconds
Record your screen at https://first-proof-isabella-week9.isazevada.chatgpt.site . Speak naturally. All employers, interviews and data shown are fictional. This script does not claim a completed real pilot.

0:00-0:35 | Conditions. Show the demo label, role, pay, criteria, capacity and time limits. Say: “This is First-proof. It addresses the problem of having abilities but no first opportunity to show them. This demo starts with an employer commitment before asking the applicant to prepare anything. The company and opportunities here are fictional.”

0:35-0:55 | Change 'Probar otra condición' to no commitment. Show blocked preparation. Return to valid. Say: “When there is no commitment or capacity, the system stops before asking for work. This is how I address the shadow: keeping people busy without a real next step.”

0:55-1:35 | Click 'Usar ejemplo ficticio'. Read the stock example, leave unverified, tick fictional confirmation and organize. Say: “The applicant reuses an experience they already have. It can come from university or a family business. The calculation is reproducible, but an unverified improvement is not presented as a proven result. The AI suggestion is simulated and labeled; it organizes evidence but does not make the decision.”

1:35-2:15 | Show reviewer role-play. Check the three criteria. Enter: 'La persona detecta una diferencia de dos unidades, explica su aportación y no afirma una mejora sin respaldo.' Confirm. Say: “This panel lets me demonstrate the employer review. In a real pilot it would belong to an authorized reviewer, not the applicant. The decision requires specific reasons and the system checks commitment and capacity again.”

2:15-2:45 | Show outcome and download. Say: “The output is a reusable proof card and a simulated next step. An interview is counted separately from an offer or paid work. I also tested a duplicate reservation bug: the first version could reserve twice, and the corrected decision logic blocks it.”

2:45-3:00 | State honest limits. Say: “This is a working fictional-data slice, not evidence of hiring impact. The remaining steps are a live LLM integration if required, a fresh-chat persona test, and real employer commitments before recruiting anyone.”

3:00-3:30 | Personal reflection. Adapt to what you actually think: “At first, I thought a better portfolio would solve the problem. My own experience changed that: I needed someone to open the door before my evidence could matter. Building the commitment check made that difference concrete. I also learned that a convincing output can still overclaim. Separating a demonstrated skill, a verified result, an interview and a job helps prevent this product from creating more hope without real progress.”
